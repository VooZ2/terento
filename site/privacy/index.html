Site and data

What happens on the public page.

What this site does not do

The website does not provide an account, login, or require an email address. It does not receive device state or Terento manifests; those remain on your Mac.

libmtp and libusb are used by the native macOS app, not by this website, and are not involved in the website’s cookies, analytics, or other website data processing.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare provides services used to deliver, secure, and operate this website, including DNS, HTTPS/TLS, content delivery, and protection against abusive traffic. Depending on the configuration, Cloudflare may process IP addresses, request metadata, and security-related information, and may set security cookies such as __cf_bm or cf_clearance. Cloudflare’s privacy policy applies. Data may be processed outside Lithuania or the EU under Cloudflare’s service configuration.

Umami analytics

The site loads the Umami analytics script from stats.enduristas.lt only after you consent, to measure visits. Umami does not use tracking cookies or create personal profiles. Visit statistics are processed on the basis of your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR.

It may record the page path, referrer, browser and device type, and country-level context to produce aggregated statistics. It is not used for advertising or personal profiling. See the Umami FAQ.

Language preference

When you choose a site language, the site stores the preference as terento-language in your browser’s local storage. This is a user-requested preference, not advertising tracking.

Cookies — what applies

This site does not use advertising or profiling cookies. Umami visit statistics do not use tracking cookies and are enabled only after your consent. The language preference is stored in localStorage only when you choose a language. Cloudflare security cookies, if used, serve infrastructure and security purposes, not marketing.

If you do not consent, Umami is not loaded and no visit statistics are sent to the analytics service. You can change or withdraw your analytics consent at any time using the privacy settings control.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may have rights to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing, object, and lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Lithuania that is the State Data Protection Inspectorate (VDAI).

To exercise these rights, contact the data controller at privacy@terento.app. Nothing here limits those rights.

The controller relies on legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR to deliver, secure and protect the public website, prevent abuse, and maintain network and information security. Visit statistics are processed only with your consent under Article 6(1)(a) GDPR for aggregated audience measurement, not advertising or personal profiling.
