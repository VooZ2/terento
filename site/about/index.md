# A free map app, made by one Garmin hiker

Terento puts free community maps on Garmin watches and Edge bike computers. It’s a free, open-source Mac app, plus a web installer that runs in your browser.

[Download](https://terento.app/download/)

## Why I made it

I’m Gediminas. I use a Garmin and love hiking in the mountains. Before a trip, I want my watch ready with maps of where I’m going, and getting them onto it took more effort than it should.

I built Terento to make that easier, for me and for anyone else with a Garmin. It’s free and open source because I want it to be useful to the community.

## What Terento is, and what it isn’t

### What it is

- A free Mac app for Apple Silicon Macs with macOS 13 or later.

- A [web installer](https://terento.app/install/) for Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera or Brave on a Mac, with Linux still in testing.

- A way to install, update and remove free maps on Garmin watches and newer Edge bike computers with map support.

- Room for your own compatible map (.img) too. Your own maps don’t update automatically.

- Free to use, with no account.

### What it isn’t

- **A Garmin product.** Garmin doesn’t make, endorse or certify Terento.

- **A map maker.** The maps come from the community projects listed below.

- **A navigation app.** You navigate on your Garmin, as you always do.

- **An app for Windows, phones or tablets.**

- **A risk to your original Garmin maps.** Terento never touches them.

- **A promise that every Garmin works.** [Compatibility](https://terento.app/compatibility/) lists the models people have already installed maps on.

## Open code, community maps

Terento’s code is public on [GitHub](https://github.com/VooZ2/terento) under the GPL-3.0 licence. You can read it, report a problem or help improve it.

The maps are made by [OpenTopoMap](https://garmin.opentopomap.org/), [Freizeitkarte](https://www.freizeitkarte-osm.de/), [MapRando](https://ravenfeld.gitlab.io/open-garmin-map/), [BBBike](https://garmin.bbbike.org) and [OSMapaPL](https://osmapa-garmin.las.org.pl/), from data by [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Terento gets each map from its original project, and the licences and credits stay with them.
