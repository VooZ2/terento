# Install free maps on your Garmin device

Terento puts free maps from OpenStreetMap projects on Garmin watches and Edge bike computers. Use the Mac app, or install straight from your desktop browser. Free and open source.

[Download for Mac](https://terento.app/download/) [Install from your browser](https://terento.app/install/)

Not sure about your model? [Check compatibility](https://terento.app/compatibility/)

Garmin watch showing the OpenTopoMap map of Lake Königssee in the Bavarian Alps

## How it works

1. Connect

Terento recognizes your Garmin and shows when it is ready.

2. Install

Choose maps from the catalog, or add your own compatible .img map.

3. Done

Your maps are installed and ready to use on your Garmin.

Terento showing third-party map providers and regions on macOS

## Manage maps

See what's installed, update maps when newer releases are available, and remove third-party maps managed by Terento. Original Garmin maps remain protected.

## Use the Mac app or your browser

### Mac app

Install catalog maps or your own .img map, see what is on your Garmin and update maps when newer releases come out.

## Questions and answers

Which Garmin watches and Edge bike computers work with Terento?

See the [Compatibility page](https://terento.app/compatibility/) for exact Garmin models and variants with at least one successful shared installation. A missing model is not necessarily unsupported. Newer Garmin Edge bike computers with map support, such as Edge 540, 840 and 1050, work too. [Read the Edge guide.](https://terento.app/guides/install-garmin-maps-edge-mac/)

Can I install third-party maps on a Garmin watch from a Mac without Garmin Express or BaseCamp?

Yes. Terento does the whole job on your Mac: pick a map and a region, install it on your Garmin, and update or remove it later. You don’t need Garmin Express or BaseCamp. [Read the installation guide.](https://terento.app/guides/install-garmin-maps-mac/)

Can I add my own .img map?

Yes. Add a compatible .img map from your Mac and install it on your connected Garmin watch.

Can I update maps later?

Yes. Terento tells you when a newer version of an installed map is out and updates it for you.

Why isn’t my Garmin watch showing up on my Mac?

Connect the watch directly with a USB cable that supports data, unlock it and wait up to 2 minutes. If it still doesn’t appear, quit Garmin Express, Android File Transfer, OpenMTP and other apps that can use the watch.

What should I do if installation fails?

On the failed installation screen, choose “Report issue”. Terento opens GitHub with the report already filled in. Review it before posting: GitHub issues are public. For help by email, include your watch model, map region and what happened. [Read the troubleshooting guide.](https://terento.app/guides/troubleshooting/)

[Open an issue](https://github.com/VooZ2/terento/issues/new/choose) [Email support](mailto:hello@terento.app?subject=Terento%20installation%20issue)

Can I install maps without installing the app?

Yes. The [web installer](https://terento.app/install/) installs, updates and removes catalog maps from Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Opera or Brave on a Mac, and on Linux, where it is still being tested. It does not work on Windows, phones or tablets yet, and it cannot add your own .img maps.
