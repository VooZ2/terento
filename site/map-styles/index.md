# Free maps for your Garmin

OpenTopoMap, Freizeitkarte, MapRando, BBBike and OSMapaPL are free topographic maps made from OpenStreetMap, for Garmin watches and Edge bike computers. See how each one draws the same place, then install the one that fits your trips.

## OpenTopoMap

For hikes where terrain matters.

A topographic map with shaded relief, so ridges and valleys stand out, and detailed roads and paths.

Covers
Countries and regions worldwide.
Contour lines
An optional add-on you choose when installing, offered for most regions. It uses extra storage.
Updates
New editions a few times a year.
Licence
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Map style © OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA).

The Freizeitkarte map of the High Tatras

## Freizeitkarte

For walks, bike rides, and days out.

A bright leisure map with walking and cycling routes, places to visit, bus stops and train stations.

Covers
Countries across Europe, including the Canary Islands, the Azores and Madeira.
Contour lines
Included.
Updates
New editions every two to three months.
Licence
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Map © Freizeitkarte (FZK project).

The MapRando map of the High Tatras

## MapRando

For exploring smaller trails on foot.

A hiking map that also draws the small paths beyond the main trails. Some labels are in French.

Covers
Countries and regions in Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia and Australia. Some countries are split into regions or states.
Updates
New editions bring in OpenStreetMap changes.
Licence
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). MapRando by Alexis Lecanu.

The BBBike map of the High Tatras

## BBBike

Weekly-updated regional maps for cycling and walking, also where other maps are missing.

Regional maps in three styles.

- Ontrail

Compact maps with smaller roads and paths that save space on your device. Supports navigation along mapped roads and paths.

- OpenTopoMap look

Trails, paths and land cover in the familiar OpenTopoMap look, without contour lines.

- Classic

The original BBBike style, still offered for a few regions.

Covers
Regions worldwide, including countries no other map here covers.
Updates
Every week.
Licence
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Garmin maps by BBBike; the OpenTopoMap look is CC BY-SA.

## OSMapaPL

For trips across Poland, on foot or by bike.

A detailed map of Poland in three styles: with Polish letters, without them, or a lighter, smaller map.

Covers
Poland, with or without the border areas.
Contour lines
A separate package.
Licence
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). OSMapaPL by OSMapa volunteers.

## Questions about the free maps

Are these maps free?

Yes. Every map here is free and made from OpenStreetMap by community projects. Terento, the app that installs them, is free and open source too.

Do they work on Garmin Edge bike computers?

Yes, on newer Edge bike computers with map support, such as the Edge 540, 840 and 1050, and on Garmin watches with map support. The [Compatibility page](https://terento.app/compatibility/) lists the models people have installed maps on.

Will a free map replace my Garmin maps?

No. The new map is added next to the maps already on your device. Your original Garmin maps are never changed or removed, and you can turn each map on or off in the device’s map settings.

Do the maps show contour lines?

Freizeitkarte includes them. OpenTopoMap offers them as an optional add-on, and OSMapaPL as a separate package. The BBBike OpenTopoMap look has none.

How do I install one?

[Download](https://terento.app/download/) Terento for a Mac with Apple Silicon, or use the [web installer](https://terento.app/install/) in a Chromium browser. Connect your watch or Edge, choose a map and a region, and install. The [Garmin watch guide](https://terento.app/guides/install-garmin-maps-mac/) and the [Garmin Edge guide](https://terento.app/guides/install-garmin-maps-edge-mac/) walk you through it.
