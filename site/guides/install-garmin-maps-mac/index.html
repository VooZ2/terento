Mac installation guide

How to install third-party maps on a Garmin watch from a Mac

Installing third-party maps on a Garmin watch from a Mac can involve MTP, manual file transfers or older desktop tools. Terento provides a native Apple Silicon workflow: connect your watch, choose a region, review storage and install.

A quick check

Before you start

  • Apple Silicon Mac
  • macOS 13 or later
  • Garmin smartwatch with map support
  • USB cable that supports data transfer
  • Latest Terento beta
  • Enough free storage on the watch

The Compatibility page shows exact models and variants with real installation evidence. It is not a complete list of every Garmin watch that may work. After connection, Terento checks the detected device and tells you whether installation can safely continue.

The workflow

Install in five steps

  1. 01

    Download Terento

    Download the latest notarized beta, open the DMG and move Terento to Applications.

    Apple Silicon and macOS 13 or later are required.

  2. 02

    Connect your Garmin

    Connect the watch with a USB data cable. Garmin watches that use MTP may take 1–2 minutes to appear, so keep the device connected and give Terento time to finish detection.

    Close Garmin Express, OpenMTP or another app that may already be using the watch.

  3. 03

    Choose a region

    Choose the region you need. Terento shows the download size, current free space and projected storage after installation.

  4. 04

    Review and install

    Review the selected maps and storage impact, then start the installation. Keep the watch connected until Terento finishes transferring and verifying the map.

    Terento checks the map source, available storage, completed transfer and final result.

  5. 05

    Check the result

    When Terento reports that the installation is complete, safely eject the watch and confirm that the map is available on the device.

    Map visibility and activity settings may differ by Garmin model. If compatibility sharing is enabled, the result helps confirm compatibility for the exact model and variant.

The watch stays protected while Terento checks the source, storage, transfer and result.

Need a hand?

If something does not work

Terento does not detect the watch

Wait up to 1–2 minutes after connecting. If the watch still does not appear, reconnect it, check that the cable supports data and close other Garmin or MTP applications.

Installation failed

Open a GitHub issue or email the diagnostic log to hello@terento.app. The log contains the information needed to investigate the failure, so you do not need to inspect device folders manually.

Open an issue → Email the log →

Not enough storage

Choose a smaller region or remove a Terento-managed map you no longer need.

The map was installed but is not visible

Reconnect or restart the watch and check its map settings. If the map still does not appear, open an issue or email the diagnostic log to hello@terento.app.

A little context

Why a native Mac workflow matters

Garmin currently describes BaseCamp for Mac as intended for Intel-based Macs and says Rosetta may enable it on Apple silicon. Garmin gives similar guidance for Garmin Express. Apple explains how Rosetta lets Intel apps run on Apple silicon. Terento is native for Apple Silicon and focuses specifically on installing and managing third-party maps.

Terento does not replace Garmin Express for official device updates or BaseCamp for broader route-planning workflows.
